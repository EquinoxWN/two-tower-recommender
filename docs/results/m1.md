## What the numbers say

- **The towers learn something personal.** Recall@10 of 0.083 against 0.048 for the most
  popular unseen movies (+73%), and NDCG@10 of 0.050 against 0.029. At 500 candidates, the
  retrieval stage's real job, the towers keep 78.9% of the held-out movies against 59.5%.
- **The popularity correction decides the outcome.** Without logQ the same model reaches only
  0.036 recall@10, below popularity: in-batch negatives push popular movies down for most users,
  while popularity alone already scores 0.048 on this data. Subtracting log q undoes that bias.
- **Retrieval fits easily in a 50 ms budget.** Finding the top 500 among 3,883 items takes about
  0.25 ms (p50) with exact search. HNSW is slower at this size and finds 98.1% of the same items;
  it starts to pay off with catalogs that are orders of magnitude larger (M3, MovieLens 25M).
- **Reproducible:** two runs gave identical losses and metrics; latency differs between runs by
  a few tenths of a millisecond.
- **Not yet measured:** the ranker and the full request path (M2 and M3).
